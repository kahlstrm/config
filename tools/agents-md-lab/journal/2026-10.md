## Decisions

- Retired from `config/AGENTS.md`: `user-can-overrule`, `incremental-edits`,
  `commit-convention-check`, `ci-tests-only`, `follow-conventions`, `comments`,
  `reuse-existing`, `root-cause-first`, `run-diagnostics`.
- Kept despite no measured effect: `vm-credentials` and `vm-no-merge` are
  security boundaries the scenarios cannot pressure-test; removing
  `vm-declarative-fix` and `vm-nix-shell` would leave `vm-tool-lifecycle`
  without context.
- `aws-profile` and `iac-first` comply without any instructions but fail when
  only their own rule is removed; the rest of the prompt pushes agents to act
  sooner. They still held with `run-diagnostics` removed.
